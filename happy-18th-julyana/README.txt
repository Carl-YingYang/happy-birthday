HAPPY 18TH, JULYANA — RESTORED WEBSITE PACKAGE

The package includes:
- index.html (the one-page birthday site; inline fallback copies are embedded)
- assets/garden-arch.webp (transparent floral garden artwork)
- assets/cake-preview.webp (the cake preview image you supplied)
- photos/julyana-01.jpg (portrait)
- photos/julyana-02.jpg (close-up portrait)
- photos/julyana-03.jpg (group memory)

The three portraits appear as separate scroll-revealed memories. Images fill their frames responsively using object-fit: cover. The garden artwork is visible behind the scenes and darkened only gently for legibility.

The cake preview always appears locally. The Explore the cake in 3D button opens the Sketchfab Holiday Cake viewer in a responsive modal. The candle-blowing button remains separate and works without intercepting 3D viewer controls.

To preview: open index.html in a modern browser.
To publish: extract the ZIP and upload the entire happy-18th-julyana folder to Netlify Drop.
The standalone HTML is also safe to open on its own because inline fallback images are embedded.
